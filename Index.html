A Journey of Memories, Loss, Growth & Faith

Suddenly, I found myself in this world in 2007. Perhaps the most interesting part of my story is that I have spent almost my entire life growing up at my maternal grandparents' home, surrounded by my grandmother and uncles. Even after 19 years, I am still growing up in the same place where my journey began. Sometimes, I feel that this has been one of the greatest blessings Allah has given me.

I do not have many special childhood memories with my parents. Almost all my childhood memories, emotions, and experiences are connected to my grandparents' home. That is where I grew up, where I learned my first lessons about life, and where most of my memories were made.

As time passed, I slowly began to grow, learn, understand, and realize the deeper meaning of life. During those early years of growing up, one question constantly occupied my mind: Why did I have to grow up away from my parents? I tried countless times to find an answer, but I never truly succeeded.

Around 2020, I completed my primary education and stepped into secondary school. Unexpectedly, the COVID-19 pandemic arrived and changed almost everything. That period became one of the most painful chapters of my life. During that difficult time, I lost someone who genuinely cared for me, someone who was like a second mother to me. Her absence left a place in my life that can never truly be replaced.

Eventually, the pandemic came to an end, and I continued my education. However, my academic journey was not easy. I struggled to make progress and often found myself moving forward without knowing the right direction. But with proper guidance, continuous effort, and determination, I eventually achieved a good result in my SSC examination. The happiness I felt on that day is something I will never forget.

And just like that, almost 19 years of my life passed in the blink of an eye.

Looking back at these 19 years, I have come to realize something truly important: Never give up, no matter how difficult your circumstances become. Life does not always happen the way we expect, and sometimes we cannot understand why certain things happen to us. But I believe that whatever Allah allows us to face has a purpose, even when we fail to recognize it at the moment. What once feels like a hardship may eventually reveal itself as a hidden blessing.

Perhaps the life I have lived, the people I have lost, the challenges I have faced, and the experiences that have shaped me were all part of a greater plan.

I am still learning, still growing, and still discovering who I am. My journey is far from over, and I have many dreams to achieve and many things to accomplish.

My philosophy is simple:

Be strong. Keep learning. Never give up. Trust Allah's plan.
